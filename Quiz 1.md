# Quiz: food

## Question

**Which of the following is a staple carbohydrate source in many Asian diets?**

1. Rice
2. Bread
3. Pasta
4. Quinoa

**Correct answer:** Rice
**Difficulty:** easy

## Question

**What is the primary source of protein in a traditional Mediterranean diet?**

**Difficulty:** medium

**What is the term for the process by which a country's central bank controls the supply of money?**
